How it works

1. Listen

Reads messages in English, Tamil or Hindi. Voice and face signals are planned for the full build.

2. Score

Each message updates a running 0–100 distress score.

3. Support

Music, breathing and games are matched to your level.

4. Escalate

In the risk zone, SMS, IVRS and counsellor follow-up are triggered.
